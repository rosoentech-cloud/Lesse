Common questions

Which citation styles do you support?

APA (including 7th edition), MLA, Chicago (notes-bibliography and author-date), and Harvard.

Do you confirm that the sources exist?

We check that each reference is complete and matches the style guide. Confirming that a source supports your claim is part of your own research.
