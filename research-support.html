What is included

  • Literature searches with a stated search strategy
  • Annotated bibliographies and summaries of sources you provide
  • Structured reading notes and thematic maps
  • Basic data cleaning and descriptive analysis, with the method documented

What we do not do

  • Writing your assessment, thesis, or paper for submission
  • Fabricating or altering sources or data

Common questions

Will you find sources for my topic?

Yes. We run a search with a stated strategy, list the databases used, and summarize the sources that fit your question. You decide which ones to read and cite.

Can you summarize articles I already have?

Yes. Send the articles, and we return structured notes with the main argument, method, and findings for each one.
