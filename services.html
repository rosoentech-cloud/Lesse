Services

What you can hire us for

Each service lists what is included and where our boundaries are. If your project falls outside these, describe it in a brief and writers will tell you whether they can help.

Research support

Help organizing sources and understanding existing work, so you can do the thinking yourself.

Included

  • Literature searches with a stated search strategy
  • Annotated bibliographies and summaries of sources you provide
  • Structured reading notes and thematic maps
  • Data cleaning and basic descriptive analysis, with the method documented

Not included

  • Writing your assessment, thesis, or paper for submission
  • Fabricating or altering sources or data

Editing and proofreading

Feedback and corrections on work you have written.

Included

  • Copyediting for grammar, clarity, and consistency
  • Developmental feedback on structure and argument
  • Tracked changes with notes explaining major edits
  • Final proofread before submission

Not included

  • Rewriting large sections so the content is no longer yours

Citations and formatting

Consistent references and document layout in the style your field or publisher requires.

Included

  • APA, MLA, Chicago, and Harvard formatting
  • Reference list checks against the style guide
  • In-text citation and figure/table numbering review
  • Template setup for journal or department requirements

Not included

  • Adding citations to sources you have not read or checked

Proposals and grants

Clear, structured documents for funders, clients, and internal approval.

Included

  • Concept notes and letters of intent
  • Full proposals against a funder's template
  • Budget narratives that match your numbers
  • Review of a draft against the call's criteria

Not included

  • Inventing outcomes, partners, or figures you cannot support

Business copy

Website pages, emails, and case studies that fit how your organization talks.

Included

  • Website and landing page copy
  • Email sequences and newsletters
  • Case studies and company profiles
  • Brand voice guide drawn from your existing material

Not included

  • Product claims or testimonials you cannot back up

Resumes, CVs and cover letters

Documents built around a specific role or application, not a generic template.

Included

  • Resume and CV rewrites based on your experience
  • Academic CVs with publications and grants formatted correctly
  • Tailored cover letters for a job description
  • LinkedIn summary edits

Not included

  • Experience or credentials you do not have

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the work in a brief. We will match you with writers who handle that kind of project.
