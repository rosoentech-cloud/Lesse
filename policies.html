Policies

How we work

These rules apply to every client and writer on Lesse. Read them before you post a project or accept one.

Academic integrity

Lesse provides research organization, editing, formatting, and feedback. We do not provide work to be submitted as a student's own assessment where the institution prohibits outside help.

Clients are responsible for following their school's or program's rules. Writers may decline any project they believe breaks those rules, and may ask for proof of permission to proceed.

Use of AI tools

Writers may use software tools as part of their process, including spelling and grammar checkers and, where the client allows it, AI assistants. Each brief states whether AI tools may be used and whether they must be disclosed.

A writer must not present AI-generated text as their own original writing when the client has said that is not acceptable. Writers must check every source they cite. Invented or unverifiable citations are grounds for removal from the platform.

Quality and revisions

Each quote states the scope, the number of revision rounds, and the review window. Work that does not match the agreed scope can be revised at no extra cost, within the stated revision rounds.

If you and a writer disagree about whether work meets the scope, contact us. We will review the brief, the quote, and the delivered work with both parties.

Payments and refunds

Payment is made per milestone. Funds for each milestone are released when you approve it, or when the review window in your quote closes without a response.

If a writer does not deliver a milestone, you are refunded for that milestone. If a project is cancelled before work starts, any amount paid is returned.

The payment methods we accept are listed on our Payments page, and each written quote confirms the method for that project.

Prohibited work

  • Plagiarized material, or work copied from another writer's or client's files
  • Fabricated sources, data, or testimonials
  • Documents designed to impersonate a real organization, person, or official record
  • Harassment of any person, including anyone named in a brief

Privacy

We collect only what we need to match you with writers and process your project: your contact details, the brief, and any files you share. We do not sell personal information.

To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, email info@rosoen.com.
